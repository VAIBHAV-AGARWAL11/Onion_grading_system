Project Overview

About the SIH Initiative

Building an automated, non-destructive optical grading system to standardize onion sorting across Indian agricultural supply chains.

Purpose of the Project

Post-harvest storage losses for onions in India frequently reach 25% to 30% due to sprouting, rot, and delayed identification of defective bulbs. This project delivers an automated, objective evaluation tool to prevent infected onions from spoiling bulk storage lots in APMC mandis and cold warehouses.

How AI & Vision are Used

The system employs deep convolutional neural networks (CNNs) trained on thousands of onion samples across varied cultivars. It segments the bulb, evaluates outer tunic uniformity, identifies early shoot emergence at the neck, and detects micro-fungal patches (Aspergillus niger) invisible to casual manual inspection.

Benefits for Quality Assessment

Automated grading eliminates human subjectivity and sorting fatigue. In under two seconds per sample, operators receive standard quality metrics, enabling transparent mandi price discovery for farmers, uniform export packaging, and targeted separation of perishable batches.
